## What WiseYield Is

For farmland owners, managers, and consultants who make high-stakes calls on incomplete information, **WiseYield is the AI agricultural-intelligence platform** that turns scattered data — soil, climate, market, field — into clear, explainable next actions.

Unlike spreadsheets that only store data, or consultants who visit monthly, WiseYield is current, connected, and always on.

## Deterministic First, AI Second

Most agricultural AI asks a language model what to plant. We don't.

Published agronomic science computes the numbers. The AI explains them.

| Layer | Grounded in |
|---|---|
| Soil | ISRIC SoilGrids, merged **per property** with your own lab reports — measured beats modelled, never a wholesale overwrite |
| Water | FAO-56 crop coefficients, reference evapotranspiration, and leaching-aware salinity |
| Climate | Köppen classification and station-derived normals for your coordinates |
| Yield | Curated regional data plus FAOSTAT national statistics, age-ramped for tree crops |
| Rotation | Botanical-family break scoring against your own planting history |
| Economics | Your costs and prices — we never invent a figure to make a projection work |

The model never picks the domain values. It reads the deterministic scores and writes the reasoning. When an input is missing, we say so instead of filling the gap with a confident guess.

## What You Get

**Decide with confidence.** Every recommendation shows its data basis and a confidence level, so you act on evidence rather than guesswork.

## Every Number Shows Its Work

Farm decisions are expensive to get wrong, so we make our own confidence legible instead of asking you to take it on faith.

- **Trust badges** on every recommendation — `data-driven` (authoritative sources), `data-assisted` (data plus AI interpretation), or `ai-generated` (AI only).
- **A reliability score, 0–100**, composed from grounding quality, source credibility, model agreement, calibration history, and freshness. It is an evidence composite, not a measured accuracy figure, and we don't dress it up as one.
- **Multi-provider cross-check** (OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google) on Grove and above — an agreement check on the *reasoning* behind a recommendation. It is not a second source for the recommendation itself, and we won't claim it is.
- **Market-price forecasts that keep learning** — every logged harvest and sale feeds back into calibration, so the forecasts sharpen season after season.

**AI suggests, you decide.** Decades of on-farm experience is the input we respect most, not the thing we're trying to replace.
